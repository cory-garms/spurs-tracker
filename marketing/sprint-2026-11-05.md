## Exit criteria and decisions

- Analytics must pass the actual-domain test before reporting measured traffic
  or attributing visits to social. If blocked, continue content and qualitative
  feedback; disclose the gap in pitches and do not claim conversion performance.
- Complete 14 daily distribution sessions, with a target of 10–14 X posts, four
  visual posts, two weekly analyses, and up to two Reddit contributions if rules
  and community response support them. Secondary channels are optional.
- Six verified initial approaches is the controllable sales target. Two calls
  and a requested proposal are stretch outcomes. No-response does not equal rejection.
- Review strongest channels by attributed sessions and engagement, not raw
  follower count alone. With tiny samples, collect another week before concluding.
- On Nov 18, select a next sprint: fulfill a pilot if agreed; otherwise refine
  positioning and approach the next five qualified prospects using actual results.
- A signed pilot starts a separate implementation task for sponsor placements,
  campaign tracking, and fulfillment. This planning sprint does not claim those
  features or a newsletter are already implemented.
