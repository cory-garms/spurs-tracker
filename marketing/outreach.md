# Sponsor outreach drafts and pipeline

Owner/sender: Cory. Status: unsent templates. Use six personalized approaches
during the sprint, including Ledger if a verified business route is available.
Replace every bracketed field; do not fabricate familiarity or metrics.

## Qualification and tracking

A qualified prospect has a named business, audience-fit rationale, verified
public business route or warm introduction, responsible role, source/date, and
specific next action. The five local placeholder rows in prospects.csv do not
count until replaced. Recheck corporate roles and routes before sending.

Public statuses: `placeholder`, `research`, `qualified`, `contacted`, `replied`,
`meeting`, `proposal`, `won`, `declined`, `no_response`. `won` requires an actual
agreement. Maintain sent dates, contact names, correspondence, amounts, and
follow-up dates privately. Scorecard totals count actual events only.

## Follow-ups and meeting

First follow-up: at least five business days after sending. Add one useful new
sample or measured result, briefly restate fit, and ask whether another person
handles this. Second and final unanswered follow-up: at least five business days
later. Respect declines; do not send repeated generic reminders.

For a fit call, ask: who should we reach, what action matters, what would make a
small pilot successful, who approves budget, and what timing is feasible?
Afterward, write a scoped proposal with dates, price, implementation work,
reporting, payment terms, and renewal decision date. No automatic renewal or
season-long exclusivity is assumed.
