## Success measures

- By November 18: validated production analytics; 14 daily distribution sessions;
  two weekly analyses; a sourced media kit; 10 qualified prospects; six tailored
  approaches, including a Ledger approach if a business contact route is verified.
- Stretch outcomes: two sponsor conversations and one pilot proposal requested.
  These depend on recipients and are not delivery promises.
- Within 90 days: aim for one paying pilot and a renewal-ready case study.

Follower counts, traffic, and revenue targets will be set from the first measured
baseline. Missing figures remain unknown, never zero or invented.

## Working rules

- Cory owns account access, publishing, outreach, relationship notes, and sales.
  Codex supports research, drafts, site work, and reporting when requested.
- Use public business channels. Keep correspondence, contact details, contracts,
  credentials, and raw audience exports in ignored `marketing/private/` or outside
  the repository; this repository is public. Public trackers contain aggregates
  and business names only.
- Store plans outside `docs/`, which is the generated public website. Update page
  generators before generated HTML when implementing site features.
- Drafting does not authorize sending messages. Publishing and outreach tasks
  below are Cory's manual tasks; do not schedule background posting jobs.
- Keep basketball analysis independent. Sponsor references must describe an
  actual agreement; never imply team endorsement or prospective-brand approval.
