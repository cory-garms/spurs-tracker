## Publication

La Tabla Spurs is an independent basketball analytics publication combining
original Spurs analysis with San Antonio's lotería-inspired visual culture.
Readers can explore team performance, player trends, game honors, Wemby-focused
analysis, and matchup previews. It is independently operated; no team or sponsor
endorsement is claimed.

The upcoming editorial commitment is one original weekly analysis, supported by
daily social distribution during the November 5–18 sprint. Historical publishing
consistency and audience size must be documented separately from this commitment.

## Proposed founding pilot

Working local-business price: **$500–$1,000 total for 30 days**, to be selected by
Cory after reviewing baseline and production time. This is an asking-price
hypothesis, not an established rate or sponsor commitment. Ledger or another
larger brand receives a tailored quote based on actual scope.

Proposed fixed scope:

- Four weekly original analyses published within the agreed 30-day window.
- One clearly labeled sponsor block on each analysis destination, if implemented
  before launch; one sponsor reference on each corresponding social graphic.
- One agreed destination link with campaign tags and a report within five
  business days after the pilot ends.
- Editorial independence: sponsorship does not buy statistical conclusions,
  favorable player coverage, or unsupported product claims.

Each analysis destination and technical placement must be specified before an
agreement. These are proposed features, not current sponsor inventory. Newsletter
placements, homepage takeovers, guaranteed reach, product reviews, and exclusivity
are outside this initial scope. No subscriber list is currently offered.

Success is evaluated using delivered placements and measured views/clicks, plus
any sponsor-provided offer results. Agree reporting fields before launch; never
promise sales or silently replace unavailable metrics.
